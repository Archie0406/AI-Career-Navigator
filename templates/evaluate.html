Metric Definitions
Accuracy
The proportion of correct predictions out of all predictions.
Precision (weighted)
The proportion of correct positive predictions relative to all positive predictions (weighted by support).
Recall (weighted)
The proportion of correct positive predictions relative to all actual positives (weighted by support).
F1-Score (weighted)
The harmonic mean of precision and recall (weighted by support). Balances precision and recall.
Cross-Validation
Model is trained and evaluated on 5 different splits of the training data to assess stability and avoid overfitting.

⚠️ Important Limitations & Notes
  • Small Dataset: The model is trained on only {{ eval_results.dataset_size }} records. Larger, real-world datasets would significantly improve generalization.
  • Test Set Performance: The metrics shown are from the test set (20% of data), which was not seen during model training.
  • Cross-Validation: 5-fold cross-validation on training data (80%) helps assess model stability without using test data.
  • Weighted Metrics: Precision, Recall, and F1-Score use weighted averaging to give equal importance to each sample (not each class).
  • Stratified Split: Both train/test split and cross-validation use stratification to maintain career distribution.
